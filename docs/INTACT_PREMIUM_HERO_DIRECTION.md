# Current Homepage Food Hero Direction

Current canonical rule:

- Burger, Pizza, Momo and Kebab are **intact premium food heroes**.
- They do **not** have exploded-layer, deconstruction, separate, or assemble interactions.
- Their composition follows the premium Soda hero language: large intact real food, floating real-food accents, premium editorial typography, glass feature cards, cursor parallax, levitation and food-specific atmosphere.
- Soda keeps its full 3D product interaction.
- Restaurant pages remain normal real-food image experiences only.

Permanent order:

`Intro → Burger → Pizza → Momo → Kebab → Soda → Search → Restaurants → Footer`
