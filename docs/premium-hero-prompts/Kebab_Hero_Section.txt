# Recreate this site as a single HTML file: ReserveUrTime — Kebab Hero

## What it is

A full-viewport premium charcoal-grill kebab hero. Three real metal skewers carrying photorealistic,
charred meat and vegetables float diagonally across a near-black environment. Warm firelight rises
from below; tiny embers drift upward; soft smoke moves behind the food.

The key interaction is different from Burger/Pizza/Momo: ingredients separate ALONG EACH SKEWER'S
AXIS rather than forming a vertical stack. Meat cubes, onion, peppers and tomato glide away from the
center while staying on the exact skewer trajectory. The polished metal skewer remains visible,
creating a sophisticated exploded technical-food composition.

Everything must look like real grilled food photography: caramelized edges, rendered fat, glaze,
char, moisture, metal reflections, smoke and heat.

## Stage

Right 62% of viewport is kebab stage.

Three skewers:
- Skewer A: dominant foreground, from bottom-left to top-right, ~760px long.
- Skewer B: upper-mid, opposite slight angle, ~570px, scale .78.
- Skewer C: far background, ~470px, scale .62, opacity .82.

All use real transparent photographic assets.

Each skewer is a rig rotated in screen plane:
A rotateZ `-27deg`
B rotateZ `+18deg`
C rotateZ `-12deg`

Use CSS transforms; do not fake perspective with flat gradient bars.

## Explode along skewer axis

Toggle:
`SEPARATE THE FIRE` / `ASSEMBLE`.

On explode, do NOT change the overall skewer angle.

For Skewer A:
- item positions move along local X relative to the center.
- first meat: `x:-165px`
- onion: `x:-110px`
- pepper: `x:-58px`
- center meat: `x:0px`
- tomato: `x:+66px`
- last meat: `x:+138px`
- optional end vegetable: `x:+198px`

Each item also:
- local y shift between -5 and +5px;
- local z/depth via translateZ 10–55px;
- rotateZ within ±5deg;
- scale 1.0, never shrink.
- duration 1.05, stagger .045, ease `"power3.inOut"`.

Skewers B/C can perform smaller synchronized separation:
- B offset multiplier .72.
- C multiplier .55.

Assemble reverses exactly.

## Firelight reaction

During explode:
- background amber glow increases by 15%.
- a light sweep travels along Skewer A from left to right in .7s.
- highlights on meat assets are NOT recolored; only environmental glow changes.
- embers briefly increase in speed by 20%, then settle.

## Smoke

Create 5–8 smoke plumes behind skewers:
- dark grey/brown translucent blurred gradients;
- opacity .035–.11;
- rise 180–380px;
- drift sideways 25–70px;
- duration 7–12s;
- keep smoke BEHIND food and text.

Do not cover food with grey fog.

## Embers

- 1–3px circles.
- palette `#f38b42`, `#e15b2c`, `#ffc06a`.
- opacity .18–.7.

- travel 120–500px upward.
