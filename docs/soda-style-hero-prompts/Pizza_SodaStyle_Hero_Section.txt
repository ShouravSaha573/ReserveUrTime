# Recreate this hero: ReserveUrTime — Artisan Pizza

## What it is

A full-viewport premium pizza hero with one **intact photorealistic 3D artisan pizza** floating in a
cinematic dark oven environment. The pizza remains whole at all times. It slowly rotates, levitates,
and tilts with cursor movement.

Around it, realistic 3D pizza-related props drift in layered depth: basil leaves, cherry tomatoes,
mozzarella pieces, pepperoni slices, mushrooms, olives and tiny flour particles. The nearby props are
softly repelled by the pointer.

Two upper-right glass cards switch between:
- `WOOD-FIRED CLASSIC`
- `TRUFFLE WHITE`

The switch keeps the pizza intact, changes the full theme, spins the pizza 720°, swaps to the second
real 3D pizza model/material if available, and reconfigures the floating topping props.
