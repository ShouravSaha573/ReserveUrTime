# Recreate this hero: ReserveUrTime — Fire-Grilled Kebab

## What it is

A full-viewport premium kebab hero with one **intact photorealistic 3D kebab/skewer presentation** at
the center. The main kebab never breaks apart. It levitates, tilts subtly toward the cursor and has
warm firelight grazing its surface.

Around it, realistic 3D props float in foreground/background depth: charred meat cubes, red onion,
bell pepper, tomato, parsley/coriander, lemon wedges and subtle ember particles. Nearby 3D props repel
from the pointer.

Two right-side glass cards:
- `CHARCOAL CLASSIC`
- `HERB & LEMON`

Switching modes morphs the atmosphere, spins the intact kebab 720°, swaps the real GLB/material if
available, and reconfigures the surrounding 3D ingredient props.
