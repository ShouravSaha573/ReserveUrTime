# Recreate this hero: ReserveUrTime — Gourmet Burger

## What it is

A full-viewport premium burger hero with one **intact photorealistic 3D gourmet burger** floating at
the center. The burger slowly levitates, tilts toward the cursor, and reacts with real-time camera
parallax. Around it, realistic 3D ingredients float at different depths: tomato slices, lettuce leaves,
pickles, onion rings, sesame seeds and small herb pieces. They drift continuously and are repelled by
the pointer like a soft force field.

The hero has two visual modes chosen from two glass cards at the upper-right:

- `CHARRED CLASSIC`
- `SMOKED CHEESE`

Switching modes:
- morphs the radial background palette,
- spins the intact central burger 720° with a motion-blur peak,
- swaps the main 3D burger model or material/texture if a second real model/material exists,
- causes the floating ingredient props to implode toward center, switch to the new garnish/ingredient
  set, then explode back to fresh positions,
- never breaks the burger apart.
