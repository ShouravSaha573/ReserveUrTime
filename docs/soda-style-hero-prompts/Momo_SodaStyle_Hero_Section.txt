# Recreate this hero: ReserveUrTime — Steamed Momo

## What it is

A full-viewport premium momo hero with one **intact photorealistic 3D momo plate / basket** as the
central product. Nothing is deconstructed. The centerpiece gently levitates and tilts toward the
cursor.

Around it float realistic 3D supporting props: individual steamed momos, coriander leaves, spring
onion, chili, sesame/spice fragments and a small sauce bowl. Subtle steam wisps rise from the center
and a few surrounding momos.

Two right-side glass cards switch between:
- `STEAMED CLASSIC`
- `CHILI SEARED`

The switch changes the background palette, spins the central intact momo presentation, swaps the main
real GLB if available, and makes the floating props implode/reappear around the new theme.
